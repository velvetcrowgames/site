About Us

Welcome to Velvet Crow Games, an independent game studio based in the heart of Athens, Greece. Founded in 2025, our passion is to craft engaging, high-quality mobile games that combine elegant design with pure fun. We believe that games should be more than just a pastime—they should be an adventure. **Runaway: Sky Towers** is our first step on that journey.
